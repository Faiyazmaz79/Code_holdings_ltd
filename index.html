Our Journey

How it all began

Starting Humble

Two professional engineers were working for leading Bangladeshi companies. Despite their successful careers, they dreamed of building something of their own. They were both passionate about real estate and had always wanted to establish a real estate company that would offer top-quality services to clients. One day, over a cup of coffee, they decided to take the plunge and establish their own real estate company. This is how Code Holdings came into reality.

Going Strong

Code Holdings Ltd. has continued to thrive and grow over the years, thanks to the hard work and dedication of its founding engineers and its team of skilled professionals. The company has expanded its services to include property development, real estate investment, property management, and rental services, offering a wide range of solutions to meet the diverse needs of its clients. Code Holdings Ltd. has always remained committed to its values of innovation, professionalism, and exceptional customer service.

Looking to the future

Code Holdings Ltd. remains committed to growing and expanding its services to better serve its clients. The company continues to push the boundaries of innovation and technology to deliver world-class real estate solutions, setting new standards for excellence in the industry.
